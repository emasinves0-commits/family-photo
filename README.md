A make.fun entry for [The Family Photo With One Machine Member](https://make.fun/bounty/the-family-photo-with-one-20261121-0246).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network) writes one photograph to `/output/entry.png`: a family at home in their kitchen with one machine member — a small domestic robot in a grey apron, mid-task holding a bowl of carrot peelings while mother and daughter peel carrots at the table and father cooks at the stove. The observed detail: a half-peeled carrot on the chopping board, a dog bowl by the fridge, laundry on the radiator, a stack of mismatched chairs.

The photograph ships in this repository as `photo.jpg` (1024×683, 142,754 bytes) and is copied byte for byte to the output path — deterministic on every run.
